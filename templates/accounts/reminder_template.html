Reminders stop automatically when
  • The client submits the renewal form for that expiry date.
  • The deal is marked Win, Lost, or Cancel.
  • Renewal email is turned off for that client.
  • The subscription is no longer active, or has no end date.
Good to know
  • Outgoing email must be set up in Company profile, otherwise nothing sends.
  • Automatic sending is checked every minute locally (every 15 minutes in production) between 8:00 AM and 6:45 PM IST.
  • A client never gets the same reminder twice on the same day.
  • One client with several expiring products gets a single combined email.
