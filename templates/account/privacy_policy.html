1. Introduction

Natraj Infotech Private Limited (“Natraj Infotech”, “we”, “us” or “our”) has developed Tracker365 Sales, an application for managing client subscriptions, renewals, leads, vendors, procurement, and customer support operations (the “Application”).

This Privacy Policy explains what personal data the Application handles, why it is handled, who is responsible for it, how it is protected, and the rights available to individuals whose personal data is processed through it.

Our approach to personal data is built on a set of core principles: personal data is processed lawfully and only for specified purposes; only the data necessary for those purposes is collected; data is kept accurate and up to date; it is retained no longer than required; reasonable security safeguards are applied; and responsibility for how data is handled is clearly allocated.

Please note The Application is a deployed business application, not a publicly available consumer service. Access is limited to authorised personnel of the organisation that operates a given deployment.

2. Scope of this Policy

This Policy applies to:

  • The Tracker365 Sales application and its modules, including dashboard, client subscriptions, renewals, leads, vendors, procurement, clients, products, company profile, team access, support tickets, and case lock.
  • Personal data processed within the Application, such as user account details and business contact information of clients, vendors, and leads.
  • Communications handled through the Application, including renewal notifications, support correspondence, and WhatsApp business messages.

This Policy does not apply to:

  • Other products of Natraj Infotech, including the primary Tracker365 product application, which are governed by their own terms.
  • Third-party platforms and services that an organisation may connect to the Application, which remain governed by the policies of those providers.
  • Infrastructure, networks, devices, or identity systems owned or administered by a customer organisation.

3. Deployment Models and Responsibility for Data

The Application is supplied as a deployable product rather than as a shared hosted service. It is currently operated by Natraj Infotech for its own internal business use, and it may be deployed for other organisations that wish to use it. In every case, each deployment is a separate, independent installation with its own containerised PostgreSQL database, and data is not pooled or shared between deployments.

Deployment Model Who Controls the Data Role of Natraj Infotech
Internal deployment Natraj Infotech, for its own business records Operates the deployment and determines the purposes of processing
Customer-designated deployment The customer organisation, which owns and controls all data entered into its instance Supplies, installs, configures and supports the software; acts on the customer’s documented instructions

Where the Application is deployed for a customer organisation, that organisation determines the purpose and means of processing the personal data recorded in its instance. Natraj Infotech processes such data only to the extent required to deliver, configure, maintain or support the software, and only on the instructions of that organisation.

Responsibility in customer environments Where a deployment is hosted on infrastructure owned, leased or designated by a customer organisation, that organisation is responsible for the security, administration, access control, patching, network protection and physical safeguarding of that environment.

The Application is designed for business use, and the personal data it handles is predominantly business-context information. The categories below describe what may be processed.

5. Purposes of Processing

Personal data is processed only for specified, lawful purposes connected with operating the Application:

  • To create, authenticate and administer user accounts and role-based access.
  • To record and manage client subscriptions, renewals, and associated commercial documentation.
  • To capture and progress sales leads received through email, WhatsApp or manual entry.
  • To manage vendor records, procurement rounds, quotations and price comparisons.
  • To deliver renewal reminders, confirmations, notifications and other operational communications.
  • To record, assign, escalate and resolve support tickets and track service commitments.
  • To generate reports, dashboards and business analysis for the operating organisation.
  • To maintain the security, integrity, availability and correct functioning of the Application.
  • To comply with applicable legal, regulatory, accounting and contractual obligations.

Personal data is not sold, rented, traded, or used for advertising, profiling, or behavioural targeting.

6. Lawful Basis and Consent

Processing in the Application is carried out on the basis of consent obtained by the operating organisation, or where processing is necessary for legitimate business purposes — including purposes for which an individual has voluntarily provided their personal data, the performance of a contract, and the conduct of employment-related and commercial functions.

Where consent is the basis of processing, the operating organisation is responsible for obtaining, recording and honouring that consent, and for informing individuals of the purposes for which their personal data is processed. Consent may be withdrawn at any time, and withdrawal will not affect the lawfulness of processing carried out before withdrawal.

8. Data Location and Hosting

The deployment operated by Natraj Infotech is hosted on cloud infrastructure located in India, within a virtual machine running a containerised PostgreSQL database.

Where the Application is deployed for another organisation, the same containerised architecture is used, and the data resides within the infrastructure designated by that organisation. The hosting location for such deployments is therefore determined by the customer.

Personal data is not transferred outside the deployment environment by the Application itself, except where an integration enabled by the operating organisation involves transmission to the relevant third-party provider.

9. Security Safeguards

Reasonable security safeguards are implemented to protect personal data against unauthorised access, disclosure, alteration and loss. These include:

SafeguardImplementation
Transport security All communication with the Application is served over HTTPS with enforced encryption in transit.
Storage protection Data is stored in an encrypted state within the hosting environment.
Access control Role-based access control restricts functionality and records by assigned role and company scope.
Data segregation Records are scoped to the operating company, and separate deployments maintain separate databases.
Authentication Microsoft and Google sign-in are supported and recommended, allowing organisations to apply their own identity and multi-factor policies.
Backup and export Complete data backup and download functionality is available so that the operating organisation can retain its own copy of its data.
Deployment isolation Each installation is delivered as an independent containerised deployment.
Current limitation — disclosed transparently In-application audit logging is not implemented in the current version of the Application. Organisations requiring detailed user-activity audit trails should rely on their own infrastructure, identity provider and database-level logging until this capability is introduced.

No method of electronic storage or transmission is entirely secure. While safeguards are implemented and maintained, absolute security cannot be guaranteed.

10. Data Ownership

All business data entered into a deployment of the Application, including client records, vendor records, leads, subscriptions, procurement records, support tickets and communication logs, remains the property of the organisation operating that deployment.

Natraj Infotech does not claim ownership of customer data and does not use customer data for its own commercial purposes, for product marketing, or for the development of any other product or service.

11. Artificial Intelligence

The Application does not use artificial intelligence or machine learning models, and no data held in the Application is transmitted to any artificial intelligence service or used to train any model.

If such functionality is introduced in a future version, this Policy will be updated before that functionality is enabled, and the relevant processing details will be disclosed.

12. Data Retention

Personal data is retained for as long as it is required for the purposes described in this Policy, or for as long as the operating organisation requires it for its business, contractual, accounting or legal obligations.

  • Records are retained within the deployment for the duration of the organisation’s use of the Application.
  • Retention periods within a deployment are determined by the operating organisation.
  • Upon discontinuation of a deployment, the operating organisation may export its data using the backup and download facility, after which the database may be decommissioned in accordance with the applicable agreement.
  • Data no longer required for any specified purpose or for compliance with law should be erased by the operating organisation.

14. Cookies and Session Data

The Application uses cookies that are necessary for it to function. These are limited to:

  • Authentication and session cookies that keep a signed-in user securely authenticated.
  • Security cookies used to protect form submissions against cross-site request forgery.
  • Preference cookies that remember basic interface choices.

The Application does not use advertising cookies, cross-site tracking technologies, or third-party marketing analytics.

15. Children’s Data

The Application is intended solely for business use by adult professionals acting in a workplace capacity. It is not directed at children, and personal data of children is not knowingly collected. If such data is found to have been entered, it will be deleted on becoming aware of it.

16. Personal Data Breach

In the event of a personal data breach affecting the deployment operated by Natraj Infotech, we will take prompt steps to contain and remediate the incident, assess its impact, and notify affected individuals and the competent authority as required under applicable law.

Where a breach occurs in a deployment hosted on infrastructure controlled by a customer organisation, that organisation is responsible for breach assessment and notification. Natraj Infotech will provide reasonable technical assistance and, where a breach is identified in the software itself, will notify affected organisations.

17. Changes to this Policy

This Policy may be updated to reflect changes in the Application, our practices, or legal requirements. The version number and effective date at the beginning of this document indicate the current revision. Material changes will be communicated to organisations operating a deployment.
